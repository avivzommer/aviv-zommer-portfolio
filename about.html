My career started in creative direction and visual communication, leading teams, building brand identities, working with major clients. For five years I built things that looked exactly right. Moving into product design shifted everything. Suddenly the work wasn't just about how things looked. It was about how systems behaved, how decisions rippled downstream, and how real people navigated complexity every day.

Since then I've worked across supply chain management, cybersecurity, cloud-native observability, and enterprise ITSM. The common thread: complex B2B products, real enterprise users, and real pressure to get things right. I've led design systems, introduced product-led growth motions, and integrated AI into design workflows.
